Privacy Policy

ThinkAhead Academy · Last updated: October 24, 2026

ThinkAhead is a non-profit learning portal that shares study notes and free books with students in Nepal. This page explains what information the website keeps and why.

What we collect

  • If you create an account with a phone number: your phone number and the password you choose, and your name if you add it.
  • If you continue with Google: the name and email address on your Google account. We do not receive your Google password, and we do not access your Gmail, contacts, Drive or any other Google data.
  • Your study activity: the notes and pen drawings you add to book pages, so they can be kept and shown again on your other devices, and which courses you are enrolled in.
  • Feedback: the rating and comment you choose to send, together with your name.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To show you the courses and books you are allowed to open.
  • To let the teacher confirm your enrollment and payment, and to contact you about your courses.
  • To improve the portal, using the feedback you send.

Who can see it

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising. Your notes and drawings are private to your account. Your details are visible to the teacher, who manages the portal.

The website runs on services that handle data for us: Supabase (database and sign-in), Google (the Continue with Google option and book file storage), and Vercel (website hosting). They process data only to provide those services.

Storage on your device

The website saves some information in your browser, such as your selected light or dark theme, your login for the current session, and a copy of your notes so they load quickly.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your account and the notes saved with it at any time. Contact the teacher on WhatsApp: +977 9826109280.

Changes

If we change how the website handles information, we will update this page and its date.
